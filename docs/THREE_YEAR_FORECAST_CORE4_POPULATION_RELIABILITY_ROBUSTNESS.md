# Population reliability blend — robustness audit

The fixed population-reliability blend with C=10,000 is the first geographic/reliability layer to improve both overall RMSE and p95 absolute error in the initial historical comparison.

This follow-up does **not** tune the blend. It audits the already fixed formula for:
- year-by-year RMSE and p95;
- number of municipalities with lower mean absolute error;
- largest municipality-level improvements and deteriorations;
- the source of the slightly higher global maximum error;
- Luleå performance.

The structural core-4 remains unchanged.
